How ATSInsight Works

From PDF parsing to actionable career guidance in four straightforward steps.

1

1. Upload Resume

Upload your resume in PDF format. We safely extract digital text using Apache PDFBox without permanent storage.

2

2. Choose Target Role

Select your intended role (Java Developer, Full Stack, Data Analyst, etc.) or optionally paste a job description.

3

3. ATS & Skill Evaluation

The system audits 9 critical ATS formatting benchmarks and compares your detected competencies against role benchmarks.

4

4. Career Guidance

Review dual scores, identify missing skills, explore verified course certifications, and download your analysis report.

Key Capabilities

Everything You Need To Stand Out

Designed with industry-standard resume best practices and student career advancement in mind.

Apache PDFBox Extraction

Extracts text, headers, and contact information accurately while detecting unreadable scanned image PDFs gracefully.

9-Point ATS Compatibility Audit

Transparent checklist grading text extractability, contact availability, section headers, word volume, and character layout.

Dual Transparent Scoring

Separate 100-point scores for ATS formatting compatibility and Role Suitability (Skills 50 pts, Projects 30 pts, Qualifications 20 pts).

Skill Gap Identification

Clear classification of matched skills and competencies not detected in your resume, avoiding inaccurate assumptions.

Authentic Certifications

Verified, official course and certification links (Oracle, Meta, freeCodeCamp, AWS) clearly distinguishing free vs paid exams.

Downloadable Executive Report

Generate and download a complete PDF report or plain-text file containing all findings, scores, and recommendations.
